Reserve my bread A night at the bakery

From 10 pm until opening time, Antoine is alone at the bakery.

Scroll down: the clock moves with you, from the starter to the first batch.

22:00

  1. 10 pm

    The starter

    Antoine feeds the sourdough starter with rye flour and warm water. It is twenty years old and was born in this bakehouse.

  2. 11:30 pm

    Mixing

    Flour from a mill forty kilometres away, water, salt, starter. The mixer turns slowly so the dough stays cool, then the dough rests until 2 am.

  3. 2:30 am

    Shaping

    Four hundred baguettes, rolled by hand one at a time, then laid in the folds of a linen cloth.

  4. 4:15 am

    The blade

    A veil of flour, then five cuts on each baguette. In the oven they will open up into ears: the grigne.

  5. 5 am

    Into the oven

    A stone-deck oven at 250 °C, twenty-two minutes for a baguette. Outside, the street already smells of bread.

  6. 5:45 am

    Out of the oven

    The bread sings as it cools: the crust crackles. It only goes into a bag once it is warm, not hot.

  7. 6:45 am

    We open

    Claire raises the shutter. First batch: baguettes, country loaves, croissants and pains au chocolat.
